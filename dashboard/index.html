What the board ranks

Every protocol whose DefiLlama record carries no token: market cap, CoinGecko id and symbol are all null. All 40 watchlist protocols are always on the board. On top of them the radar adds the 160 largest pre-token protocols below the watchlist floor of $50M TVL — the early signal the bounty exists to find. Wrapped, staked and exchange-named products and versioned pools are excluded, so the board is a launch list, not a TVL table.

Protocols that already trade are never ranked. They sit in Trading now, labelled as context, with their own market cap.

The implied value

implied = TVL × 0.26 × category tilt × momentum × maturity × breadth

Base rate
0.26 — a pre-token protocol's launch FDV as a share of the TVL it already custodies.
Category tilt
0.82× for liquid staking and bridges to 1.12× for perpetuals and derivatives, from the category string alone.
Momentum
1 + clamp(7-day TVL change, −60% to +180%) × 0.55.
Maturity
0.72 + min(days since listing, 1460) / 1460 × 0.53; unknown listing dates take 0.88.
Breadth
1 + min(chains − 1, 4) × 0.035.

Every factor is a pure function of a snapshot field. No protocol name is special-cased; each row's detail view shows its own arithmetic so the total can be checked by hand.

The farm plan

Seven steps per protocol: wallet hygiene, funding, the category's core action, a weekly qualifying action repeated for 8 to 12 weeks, a hold period of 30 to 90 days, a claim-hygiene step, and a written log with an exit rule. Capital, gas and hold time are derived from the protocol's own TVL, chain and age, and every step states its cost and its time.
